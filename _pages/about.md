I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am supervised by Professor Junxian He, who leads the group. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

I received my B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

## Research Interests

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
